# ITAME Bastions - Quick Reading Guide

This guide summarizes only the user-provided PDF. Page numbers below are PDF
page numbers, which avoids confusion with the document's printed footer
numbers.

## Fastest reading path

1. Read PDF pages 2-6 for the guild-specific assumptions, turn cadence,
   facility rules, and available orders.
2. Use PDF page 7 as the special-facility index, then jump to the desired
   facility on PDF pages 8-17.
3. Read PDF pages 17-19 before resolving a Maintain order or a Bastion event.
4. Treat PDF pages 20-26 as selected supporting excerpts, not as a complete
   replacement for the 2024 DMG or PHB.

## Executive summary

The document adapts the 2024 Bastion framework to a shared ITAM Experts guild
building. The Bastion is both a home base and a system for crafting,
researching, recruiting, harvesting, trading, and gaining other benefits.
Players with permanent characters contribute facilities to the shared
Bastion, but prerequisites still apply and only one character can use a
facility at a time.

A Bastion turn occurs every seven in-game days by default. The document
explicitly leaves the guild's actual cadence unresolved and suggests possible
alternatives such as one turn every two or three sessions or every seven
real-world days.

Each permanent character starts with two basic facilities: one Cramped and one
Roomy. Basic facilities are primarily narrative spaces. Their maximum sizes
are 4 squares (Cramped), 16 squares (Roomy), and 36 squares (Vast). New basic
facilities and enlargements cost both GP and construction time.

Special facilities produce game effects and generally arrive through level
advancement rather than purchase. The acquisition table gives 2 facilities at
level 5, 4 at level 9, 5 at level 13, and 6 at level 17. Facilities may impose
level, class-feature, proficiency, or focus prerequisites. Most include
hirelings and one or more supported orders.

The seven Bastion orders are Craft, Empower, Harvest, Maintain, Recruit,
Research, and Trade. Maintain applies to the entire Bastion, blocks other
orders for that turn, and triggers a roll on the Bastion Events table.

The master table covers 29 named facilities. PDF page 7 is the fastest
comparison view because it lists each facility's level, prerequisite, and
order. The detailed catalog contains only 26 matching descriptions:
Training Area, Trophy Room, and War Room appear in the master table but do not
have description sections in this PDF.

Bastion Events are resolved after Maintain orders. The event section includes
a d100 table, individual event descriptions, attacks and defenders, facility
shutdowns, requests, discoveries, treasure, and rules for the possible fall
of a Bastion.

The appendices collect selected referenced material from the 2024 DMG and PHB:
marks of prestige, poison, charms, magic-item crafting, tools, mounts,
vehicles, equipment crafting, selected spells, and four creature stat blocks.
The foreword warns that some referenced rules remain in the source books, so
this PDF is intentionally not self-contained.

## Decisions the guild still needs to make

- What cadence replaces or confirms the default seven in-game-day Bastion
  turn?
- How are shared facilities selected, replaced, scheduled, and assigned when
  multiple characters want the same benefit?
- Who is authorized to issue whole-Bastion Maintain orders and resolve the
  resulting event rolls?
- How are prerequisites interpreted when a facility is shared by the guild
  but only one character can use it at a time?
- Which references that still point to the DMG need to be added to make the
  packet sufficient for play?
- Should the missing detailed descriptions for Training Area, Trophy Room, and
  War Room be added, or should those facilities be unavailable?

## Extraction notes

The PDF contains embedded text on every page. The cleaned Markdown removes the
Homebrewery character spacing and duplicated decorative overprint. Complex
tables and stat blocks should still be checked against the corresponding
one-page PDF because the embedded text layer does not encode every visual
column boundary.
